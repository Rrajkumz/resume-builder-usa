GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you start building.

Is ResumeCraft free to use?

Yes. This version is free to use and does not require an account. Your browser handles the print-to-PDF step.

How do I download my resume?

Click “Print / Save PDF” or “Save PDF”, then select “Save as PDF” as the destination in your browser's print window and save the file.

Is my information uploaded anywhere?

This static builder processes the information in your browser and does not send it to a resume server. Avoid using a shared computer if you are entering personal information.

Will this guarantee an interview?

No resume can guarantee an interview. Tailor your content to each role, use accurate information, and highlight relevant achievements and measurable outcomes.

What does ATS-friendly mean?

ATS-friendly resumes use clear headings and readable text. The Classic and Minimal styles are designed to be simple, but no template can guarantee how every applicant tracking system will parse a document.
